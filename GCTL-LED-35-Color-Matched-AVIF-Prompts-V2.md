# GCTL LED — 35 Color-Matched Master Product Image Prompts (V2)

**All 35 prompts revised to match the existing black/graphite kiosk + blue/cyan screen visual language.** Each prompt specifies a **distinct product form factor** (not just a generic monitor). Product names and image filenames follow the 161-product navigation families in `src/data/nav-data.js`.

- **Output:** exactly `1200 × 900 px`, **AVIF** (`.avif`), landscape 4:3.
- **Image folder:** `public/images/product/`.
- **Hardware:** matte black / deep graphite across all product families.
- **Screen graphics:** cobalt blue / electric blue / cyan abstract flowing waves (for display products).
- **Background:** white / very pale neutral gray, professional catalog photo, full product visible.
- **Use:** Homepage product cards + navbar thumbnails. **No HTML/CSS/layout changes needed.**
- **Note:** A master image represents a product *family*, not the exact appearance of every screen size, pixel pitch, or supplier SKU. For exact model likeness, provide real manufacturer reference photos.
- **Important:** Image generators may output PNG/JPG even when AVIF is requested; convert to `.avif` afterward while preserving exact dimensions and filenames. The output format is NOT `.jpg`.
