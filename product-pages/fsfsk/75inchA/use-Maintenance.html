Preventive Maintenance

Ensure smooth 24/7 commercial operation

✓

Tempered Glass Care

Wipe the 4mm tempered protective glass using a dry microfiber cloth and non-ammonia display cleaners. Never use abrasive pads on the anti-glare finish.

✓

Firmware & App Updates

Keep the Android operating system and digital signage media players updated to improve codec decoding, network security, and system stability.

✓

Ventilation & Thermal Inspection

Clean dust from the rear intake louvers every quarter to promote unrestricted airflow and protect internal motherboard components from thermal throttling.

✓

Cable & Grounding Verification

Inspect grounded AC power lines, shielded RJ45 ports, and security lock fixtures to ensure stable electrical protection in public environments.
