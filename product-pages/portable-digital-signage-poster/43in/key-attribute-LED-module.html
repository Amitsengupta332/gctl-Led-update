Core Specifications of 43inch Outdoor Portable Battery Powered Display

Designed for high-impact outdoor advertising, the 43inch Outdoor Portable Battery Powered Display (Model: UD-55LA) by USENDA offers superior daylight visibility with 1500 to 3000 nits brightness, rugged IP65 weatherproofing, and an inbuilt industrial air conditioning system for extreme operating temperatures (-20 °C to 70 °C). Powered by LG/BOE commercial panels with 4K UHD support, Android/Windows OS, and comprehensive Cloud-Based Remote Device Management.
