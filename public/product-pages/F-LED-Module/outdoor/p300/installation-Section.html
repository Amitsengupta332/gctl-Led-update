INSTALLATION METHODS OF p3 Outdoor LED Flexible MODULE

We provide multiple installation solutions to fit different environments and project requirements. Choose the most suitable mounting structure for your outdoor LED display.

Step 01 — Prepare the Installation Surface

Ensure the mounting surface or supporting structure is clean, level, stable, and properly aligned before installation.

Step 02 — Install the Magnetic Modules

Position the 320 × 160 mm flexible LED modules on the supporting structure and secure them using the integrated magnetic mounting system.

Step 03 — Connect Signal & Power Cables

Connect the signal cables and power cables between the modules according to the LED display wiring layout, ensuring all connections are firm and correctly oriented.

Step 04 — Shape the Flexible Display

Carefully bend and arrange the flexible modules according to the required display design, such as curved, wave, column, or creative LED screen.

Step 05 — Connect Control System

Connect the LED modules to the control card, sending system, and power supply to establish proper communication and power distribution.

Step 06 — Test & Calibrate the Display

Power on the LED display and check module connectivity, brightness, color uniformity, and image performance. Complete calibration if required.

Step 07 — Final Inspection

Inspect all module connections, mounting points, cables, and display surfaces to ensure a secure, seamless, and reliable installation.
