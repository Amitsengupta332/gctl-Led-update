Installation Method

The 43" Floor Standing Kiosk Displays are designed for easy installation, allowing users to set up the displays quickly and efficiently. The installation process involves a few simple steps, including preparing the necessary mounting hardware, aligning the display with the base, securing it with screws, and connecting the power supply. This straightforward installation method ensures that businesses can deploy the displays without the need for specialized tools or technical expertise, making it accessible for a wide range of users and applications.

1

First Step

Prepare 12 screws, mounting hardware and a hexagonal screwdriver.

2

Step 2

Rotate the screen carefully and align it with the base.

3

Step 3

Tighten all 12 screws securely for maximum stability.

4

Step 4

Stand the screen upright and connect the power supply.

✅

Installation Complete

Your digital signage display is now ready for operation.
