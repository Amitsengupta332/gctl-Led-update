COB Technology

COB Technology: Normal COB vs Flip-Chip COB

Normal Formal COB

Conventional Wire-Bond
  • Gold wire creates optical obstruction, limiting luminous efficiency and peak brightness.
  • Wire bonding is vulnerable to thermal stress fracturing over extended operating cycles.
  • High thermal resistance slows heat transfer to the substrate.
Our Technology

Flip-Chip COB

Wireless Direct Bonding
  • No Wire Obstruction: 30% higher luminous efficiency and pure uniform light distribution.
  • Direct Thermal Path: Heat dissipates straight to the circuit board, keeping the screen cool.
  • Maximum Stability: Zero risk of broken bond wires; ultra-high reliability and product life.
