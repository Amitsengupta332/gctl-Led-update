Maintenance Guide

Keep your kiosk running smoothly

✓

Regular Cleaning

Use a soft, dry microfiber cloth to clean the display regularly. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive materials that may damage the screen.

✓

Software Updates

Keep the Android operating system and installed applications updated to improve performance, stability, and security.

✓

Check Connections

Periodically inspect the power supply, HDMI ports, cables, and Wi-Fi connection to ensure reliable operation.

✓

System Diagnostics

Perform routine system diagnostics to identify potential issues early and maintain smooth, uninterrupted kiosk performance.
