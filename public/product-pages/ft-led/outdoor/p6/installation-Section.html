Advanced Insulation Methods for Flexible & Transparent P6.25 Outdoor LED Film Display

The GCTL-LED Outdoor Transparent P6.25 LED Film Display features an effective insulation design that helps protect internal components and support stable, reliable operation. Its insulated construction enhances safety and durability for long-term Outdoor LED display applications.

Step-1

After Cleaning the installation area, spray the some water.

Step-2

Peel off the screen protective film and stick the screen on the installation area.

Step-3

Use a scraper to press the screen and remove any air bubbles, ensuring a smooth and secure installation.
