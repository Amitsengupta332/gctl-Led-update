Multiple Control Methods for OutdoorTransparent P4 LED Display

The GCTL-LED Outdoor Transparent P4 LED Display supports multiple control methods for convenient and flexible content management. Users can easily update images, videos, advertisements, and other digital content through WiFi, 4G, and network-based control, enabling efficient remote operation for retail stores, showrooms, exhibitions, and commercial spaces.

Synchronous Control:

Principle: Directly connect the screen to devices such as computers and video processors via cables (network cables, optical fibers, etc.) to achieve real-time synchronous display between the screen and the control terminal, with extremely low signal transmission delay.

Asynchronous Control:

Pre-import content into the screen's control card through storage devices such as U disks and SD cards. The screen reads and plays the content independently without the need for a continuous connection to the control device.
