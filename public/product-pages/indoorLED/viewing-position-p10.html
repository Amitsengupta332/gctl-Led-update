PRECAUTIONS DURING P10 Indoor LED

The area of the Indoor P10 LED display is generally larger, ranging from tens of square meters to hundreds of square meters or even much larger. Because of its high brightness and large viewing angle, Indoor P10 LED display play an important role in Indoor advertising, stations, squares, shopping malls, etc. As a form of Indoor media, LED display screen has unique advantages. Indoor media refers to the media for advertising information set up at Indoor venues such as the roofs of major buildings, the front doors of commercial areas, and roadsides.

Viewing angle

The viewing angle means the position where viewers can see the image adequately. You can measure it in horizontal and vertical directions.

If the position of LED screens is higher than horizons, the vertical viewing angle is critical. For instance, hanging or pole-mounted LED screens. Unit Customized Size Indoor Fixed P10 LED Display is a good choice. It has an ultra-wide viewing angle. In the 140/140 viewing angle, People can view the image from multiple angles.

Viewing distance of P10 LED

The viewing distance can be an appropriate distance that you can see the image on the LED screen.

You can judge the correct viewing distance not only from the pixel pitch but also the total size of the P10 LED screen. In addition, the installation of Indoor P10 LED screens also needs to consider the position of audiences. For example, the large P10 LED display beside the highway mainly provides information for vehicles, so the viewing distance should be very far.
