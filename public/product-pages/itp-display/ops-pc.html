OPS PC Module

Standard OPS PC Slot Architecture

OPS is also called mini PC, which has full functions of the PC. It has a lot of connections ports, such as VGA, HDMI, USB, Audio, LAN port and so on. It has one power port, which can connect outer power, and you can connect it to the monitor by VGA or HDMI cable. OPS can be plugged at the back of MiBoard by JAE 80 pins port, then it will become full functions of the PC.
