The 4K Touch Screen K-Type Information Kiosk is ideal for malls, retail stores, exhibitions, corporate offices, hotels, hospitals, and public facilities. It provides an interactive platform for digital signage, way finding, product information, self-service access, and customer engagement.

Interactive directory, store information, promotions

Hotel Lobby

Check-in information, facilities introduction, local guide

Airport

Flight information, wayfinding, services & navigation

Retail Store

Product information, self-service ordering, digital catalog

Exhibition Center

Event information, exhibitor directory, hall navigation

Hospital

Department directory, queue management, health information

Government Building

Public information, policy display, self-service inquiry

Restaurant
