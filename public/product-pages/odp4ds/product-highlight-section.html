What is an Outdoor P4 LED Display?

The Outdoor P4 LED Display Screen is a high-resolution digital advertising display designed for outdoor environments. With a pixel pitch of only 4mm, it delivers sharp images, vibrant colors, and clear video content even from close viewing distances. It is ideal for businesses, shopping malls, commercial buildings, educational institutions, transportation hubs, and outdoor advertising campaigns.
