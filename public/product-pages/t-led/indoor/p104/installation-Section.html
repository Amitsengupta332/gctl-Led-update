Advanced Insulation Methods for Indoor Transparent P10.41 LED Display

The GCTL-LED Indoor Transparent P10.41 LED Display features an effective insulation design that helps protect internal components and support stable, reliable operation. Its insulated construction enhances safety and durability for long-term Indoor LED display applications.

Hanging the Installation Surface

Ensure the installation surface is clean, dry, and free from dust or debris. Use a level to mark the desired position for the LED display.

Stacking the Magnetic Modules

Align the magnetic modules with the installation surface and stack them securely. Ensure that each module is properly connected and aligned for a seamless display.

Glass Mounting and Scraping

After stacking the magnetic modules, mount the glass panels onto the display. Use a scraper to ensure that the glass is securely attached and free from any gaps or misalignments.
