Flexible Installation Methods for P3 Indoor Tunnel LED Module

The GCTL-LED P3 Indoor Tunnel LED Module is designed for flexible installation and customized tunnel LED display configurations. Its modular structure supports convenient assembly, secure mounting, and efficient maintenance for reliable Indoor display performance.

Step 1: Custom Specifications

The installation begins with a detailed consultation. A project manager meets with a GCTL specialist to define the requirements, referencing technical blueprints of the curved tunnel and specifying the flexible LED modules needed for the unique geometry.

Step 2: Production and Shipping

Step 3: Welding the Steel Support Structure

With the components arriving on-site, the environment shifts to the dark construction zone inside the tunnel. Workers verify the layout and begin the heavy installation. This image captures the gritty work of actively welding the pre-fabricated steel support framework directly onto the curved concrete wall of the tunnel, precisely matching the radius defined in the initial specifications.

Step 4: Module Assembly

With the steel support structure securely in place (visible in Image 3), the critical work of mounting the display modules begins. While simple direct assembly is an option, achieving the best visual quality—specifically smoother, seamless transitions at curved corners and higher protection—is best done using custom die-cast aluminum cabinets. Image 4 shows this precise assembly method: workers carefully mount these specialized, curved cabinets to the steel structure, utilizing built-in magnetic plates to attach the flexible LED modules flawlessly.

Step 5: Cable Connections

Once the main support structure is complete and the modules are physically mounted, the system requires intricate electrical and data work. Image 5 provides a close-up, focused view of technicians performing this step. They are crouched behind the newly installed structure, meticulously connecting both signal cables (data) and power cables to the rear ports of the aluminum modules. These connections must follow the correct sequence and orientation to ensure system stability.

Step 6: Operation and Testing

The final step is the culmination of the installation process. Once the internal cabling is verified, the system is powered on for the first time. Image 6 shows the "reveal" inside the dark tunnel. The curved screen is now fully active and glowing intensely with vibrant video content, illuminating the concrete environment. The same lead technician (from Image 5) and an engineer now monitor the display's stability and current on a diagnostic laptop, making final adjustments to ensure maximum performance before the project is considered complete.
